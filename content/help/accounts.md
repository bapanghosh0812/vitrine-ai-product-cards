## Create an account
Go to **Sign up**, enter your name, email and a password of at least 8 characters with a letter and a number, then tick the box to accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Opening the Terms shows the full document; the Accept button unlocks once you scroll to the end. You can also continue with **Google**.

## Verify your email
After signing up with email we send a verification link. Open it, then return to Vitrine; the Verify email page checks again on its own. Until you verify, the Studio still works but saving to the cloud Library is paused.

## Forgot your password
On the Sign in page choose **Forgot password**, enter your email and follow the link we send. For privacy we show the same message whether or not the email has an account.

## Import guest cards
If you made cards as a guest, Vitrine offers to import them into your account the first time you sign in on that browser.

## Updated terms
When the Terms of Service or Privacy Policy change, you are asked to review and accept the new version before continuing.
