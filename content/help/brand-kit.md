The Brand Kit is sent with every generation while it is switched on.

## Fields
- **Brand name**: used naturally in copy when it fits.
- **Brand voice**: a sentence or two, for example "warm, modern Indian craft, never stuffy".
- **Words to avoid**: words the AI should never use, separated by commas.
- **Brand colours**: up to five hex colours. They tint the Luxe and Bold templates and can inspire imagery in the copy.

## Switching it on or off
Use the "Use Brand Kit" switch on the Brand Kit page or in Settings, Brand Kit. Guests can use a Brand Kit too; it is stored in the browser.
