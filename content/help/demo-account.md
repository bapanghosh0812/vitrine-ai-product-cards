Vitrine has a shared demo account so reviewers can explore every signed-in feature without creating an account.

## How to use it
Press **Try demo account** on the Sign in page or the home page. The credentials are also listed in the project README.

## Restrictions
The demo account is a normal user with a special flag. To keep it usable for everyone, it cannot change its email or password, cannot delete the account, and cannot delete its sample cards. These rules are enforced by the database security rules, not only hidden in the interface.

## Resets
Sample cards, Brand Kit and profile are restored automatically every hour, so anything you change in the demo is temporary. Please do not enter personal information in the demo account.
