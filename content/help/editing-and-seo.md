Everything the AI writes is editable. Step 3 in the Studio has three tabs: **Copy**, **Style** and **Ask AI**.

## Inline editing (Copy tab)
Edit the title, the description and the keywords in the Copy tab next to the card preview. The card updates as you type, and every change is saved to your history so you can undo it.

## SEO character counters
- **Title**: the counter turns gold above 50 characters and red above 60. Search engines usually show about 60 characters of a title.
- **Description**: the counter turns gold above 150 and red above 160 characters, the typical length of a search snippet.
- **Keywords**: 4 to 10 are recommended. Remove a keyword with its small cross, add one with the "Add keyword" field.

The counters are guidance only. Marketplace listings and social posts can use longer descriptions.

## Style tab
- **Palette:** eight two-tone palettes (Emerald & Gold, Midnight Sapphire, Bordeaux, Noir, Rose Gold, Royal Plum, Ocean Teal, Terracotta), the template's own colours, or your custom base and accent colours.
- **Typography:** serif or sans titles, three title sizes, and Title Case or UPPERCASE.
- **Layout:** sharp, soft or round corners, fill or fit for photos, and a gilded hairline frame.
- **Badge:** New, Bestseller, Limited edition, Handmade, Eco-friendly, Gift ready, or your own text (up to 24 characters).
- **Show on card:** switch the category, description, keywords or price on and off.
- **Density:** how many keywords and how many description lines appear.

Every style change can be undone, **Reset style** returns to the template defaults, and the style is saved with the card and used in PNG, JSON and HTML exports.

## Ask AI tab
Write what you want changed in your own words, for example "make it warmer and shorter, mention free shipping". **Enter adds a new line; only the Send button sends** (or `Ctrl/Cmd + Enter`). Quick refinements (Shorter, More luxurious, Warmer, Playful, Add urgency, SEO boost, Simpler words, Gift angle) apply in one click. The AI keeps the facts it was given, and Undo brings back the previous version.
