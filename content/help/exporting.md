Open the **Export** menu in the Studio toolbar.

## Formats
- **PNG**: a high-resolution image of the card exactly as you see it. Choose 1x, 2x or 3x scale in Settings, Card & Export.
- **JSON**: the title, description, keywords, category and template as structured data, handy for developers and product feeds.
- **HTML**: a self-contained, safely escaped HTML snippet with inline styles that you can paste into a store page or newsletter.
- **Copy text**: copies the title, description and keywords to your clipboard.

## Watermark
PNG exports include a small "Made with Vitrine" mark by default. Turn it off in Settings, Card & Export.
