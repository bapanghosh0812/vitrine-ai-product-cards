Vitrine turns a product name and a category into a polished product card: an AI-written **title**, a **short description** and a set of **keywords**, shown on a styled card you can edit and export.

## Create your first card
1. Open the **Studio** from the sidebar (or press `G` then `S`).
2. Type the **product name**, for example "Handwoven Banarasi silk saree".
3. Pick a **category**. Each category has its own 3D icon.
4. Press **Generate details**. In a few seconds the card appears with a title, a description and keywords.
5. Click any text on the card to edit it, or switch templates in the toolbar.

## Do I need an account?
No. Guest mode works without signing in. Your cards are saved in this browser's local storage only. Create a free account to sync cards across devices, keep a Library in the cloud, save a Brand Kit to your account and see your profile stats. When you sign in for the first time, Vitrine offers to import the cards you made as a guest.

## The main areas
- **Studio**: generate, edit, compare variations and export.
- **Library**: every card you saved, with search and favourites.
- **Templates**: six card designs, from Minimal to Marketplace listing.
- **Brand Kit**: your brand voice and colours, used in every generation.
- **Settings**: themes, generation defaults, export options, privacy and accessibility.
- **Ask Kabir**: the support assistant in the bottom-right corner.
