Each card shows either your product photo or the category's 3D icon.

## Upload a photo
Press **Add image** in the Studio toolbar. JPEG, PNG and WebP are supported, up to 10 MB before compression. Vitrine resizes the image in your browser (longest side 900 px) and converts it to WebP, so the stored copy is usually 40 to 250 KB.

## Where images are stored
Images are never uploaded to a file server. As a guest they stay in this browser. With an account they are stored inside your card in the cloud database, which is why they are compressed to stay under 300 KB.

## 3D icons
Without a photo, the card uses a 3D icon for its category (from Microsoft's open-source Fluent Emoji, MIT licence). Remove your photo at any time to go back to the icon.
