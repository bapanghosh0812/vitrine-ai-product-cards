## What we store
- **Guests**: cards, settings and Brand Kit stay in your browser's local storage.
- **Account holders**: your profile (name, avatar, consent record), cards, Brand Kit and settings are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) and protected by security rules so only you can read them.

## AI processing
The product details you type are sent to our AI providers (Groq, with Google Gemini as a fallback) to write the copy. Free tiers of AI services may use inputs to improve their services, so **do not type personal or confidential information** into product fields.

## Export your data
Settings, History & Privacy, **Export my data** downloads a JSON file with your profile, settings, Brand Kit and all cards.

## Delete your account
Settings, Account, **Delete account** permanently deletes your cards, profile and login. This cannot be undone. Guests can clear everything with **Clear local data**.

## Cookies
Vitrine uses only essential storage by default (sign-in session and your cookie choice). Preference storage (theme, sounds, defaults) is optional and can be changed any time from the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.

## Rights and contact
You can request access, correction or deletion of your data. See the Privacy Policy for details and the grievance contact.
