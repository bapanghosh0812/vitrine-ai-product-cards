Open **Settings** from the sidebar or the avatar menu. Use the search bar at the top to jump to any setting.

## Appearance
Choose **Light** (ivory), **Dark** (emerald-black), **OLED** (true black, saves battery on OLED screens) or **System** to follow your device. You can also switch theme from the top bar.

## Generation defaults
Default tone, language, number of variations, creativity and preferred AI provider (Automatic, Groq first or Gemini first).

## Card & Export
Default template, PNG export scale and the "Made with Vitrine" watermark.

## Notifications & Sound
Toasts for completed actions, subtle interface sounds and haptic feedback on supported phones.

## Accessibility
Reduce motion, larger text and stronger focus outlines. Vitrine also respects your system's reduced-motion setting.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- `Ctrl/Cmd + K`: command palette
- `Ctrl/Cmd + Enter`: generate
- `Ctrl/Cmd + Z` / `Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + Z`: undo / redo
- `G` then `S`, `L`, `T` or `B`: go to Studio, Library, Templates or Brand Kit
- `?`: show all shortcuts
