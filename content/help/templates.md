Switch templates at any time: your copy stays the same, only the design changes.

## The six templates
- **Minimal**: ivory background, generous whitespace, perfect for clean catalogues.
- **Luxe Gold**: deep emerald with gold details for premium products.
- **Glass**: frosted glass panels over soft light; great for tech and beauty.
- **Bold**: large type and high contrast for launches and sales.
- **Instagram post**: a square 1:1 layout sized for social media.
- **Marketplace listing**: a clean listing with price, keyword bullets and a buy-box style layout.

## Default template
Choose your default in Settings, Card & Export. You can also browse all templates on the Templates page and press "Use template".
