## "AI is busy" or generation failed
Wait a few seconds and press Generate again. If you see a rate-limit message, wait for the countdown. If it keeps failing, both AI providers may be down; try again later.

## Google sign-in popup closes or is blocked
Allow pop-ups for this site and try again. If the popup is blocked, Vitrine switches to a full-page redirect automatically.

## The verification email did not arrive
Check spam and promotions folders, then use **Resend email** on the Verify email page (once per minute).

## PNG export looks different
Wait until fonts and images finish loading, then export again. Very large images are scaled down for export.

## Offline
Vitrine works as an installable app. Without internet you can browse the interface and your local cards, but generation and sync need a connection. An offline page appears if a page cannot load.

## Still stuck?
Ask Kabir, or choose **Talk to a human** to open a support ticket.
