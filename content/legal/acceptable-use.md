This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") applies to everyone who uses Vitrine. It forms part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). We may update it as new risks appear.

## 1. Use Vitrine for honest commerce
Vitrine exists to help you describe real products accurately and attractively. Use it for products you are entitled to sell or promote, and make sure published copy is truthful.

## 2. Prohibited content
Do not use Vitrine to create, store or share content that:
- is illegal, or promotes illegal goods or services (for example weapons, drugs, counterfeit goods, wildlife products or stolen items);
- is false or misleading, including fake reviews, invented certifications, false health or safety claims, or deceptive pricing;
- infringes copyright, trademarks, design rights or other intellectual property, or impersonates a brand or person;
- is sexually explicit, exploits or endangers children in any way, or is hateful, harassing, violent or discriminatory;
- contains personal data of others without a lawful basis, or doxxes anyone;
- facilitates fraud, phishing, scams, spam or malware.

## 3. Prohibited behaviour
Do not:
- attempt to bypass, disable or probe safety measures, prompt instructions, content filters, rate limits or authentication;
- try to extract system prompts, API keys, configuration, source secrets or other users' data, including through prompt injection or jailbreak techniques;
- access or modify data that is not yours, or attempt to escalate privileges (for example setting roles or flags on your own account);
- use bots, scripts or scrapers to generate content at volume, resell the service, or use it to train competing models;
- overload, scan, attack or reverse engineer our systems or our providers' systems;
- use the support assistant or ticketing system to send spam, abuse or unrelated requests.

## 4. Security research
We welcome responsible disclosure. If you find a vulnerability, report it privately as described in the project's SECURITY.md file and give us reasonable time to fix it before disclosure. Do not access other users' data, degrade the service, or run automated scans against production without written permission.

## 5. Enforcement
We may investigate suspected violations and may remove content, rate-limit or block requests, suspend or terminate accounts, and report unlawful activity to the authorities. Where reasonable we will tell you what happened and how to appeal.

## 6. Reporting abuse
Report misuse to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} or through a support ticket. Include links, ticket IDs or screenshots where possible.
