This Privacy Policy explains how Vitrine handles personal data. It is written to meet the requirements of India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** ("**DPDP Act**") and, where they apply, the **EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation** ("**GDPR**").

**Summary.** We collect only what we need to run Vitrine: your account details, the cards and settings you save, and minimal technical logs. Product details you type are sent to AI providers to write copy. We do not sell data, show ads or use tracking cookies. You can export everything or delete your account at any time.

The data fiduciary (under the DPDP Act) and controller (under the GDPR) is **Bapan Ghosh**, an individual developer based in Kolkata, West Bengal, India, who operates Vitrine. Contact: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}, or the project's GitHub Issues page for non-confidential matters.

## 2. Data we collect
**a. Account data (only if you create an account).** Name, email address, password (stored only as a salted hash by Google Firebase Authentication, never visible to us), sign-in provider, email-verification status, account creation and sign-in timestamps. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo URL from Google.

**b. Consent record.** The versions of the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy you accepted and the time you accepted them.

**c. Content you create.** Product names, categories and optional details (audience, tone, features, price, language), the generated titles, descriptions and keywords, your edits, chosen templates, favourites, Brand Kit (brand name, voice, words to avoid, colours), and images you upload (compressed in your browser).

**d. Profile and preferences.** Avatar choice, settings (theme, generation defaults, export and accessibility options) and usage counters (cards generated, saved and exported) shown on your profile.

**e. Support data.** Questions you ask Ask Kabir (sent to our server to generate an answer), and support tickets you create, including subject, message, an optional chat transcript, the page you were on and your account email.

**f. Technical data.** IP address and request metadata processed transiently by our hosting providers and our API for security and rate limiting, and minimal server logs (event type, provider used, response time, request ID). Our logs do not store the text of your inputs or outputs.

**g. Guest mode.** If you use Vitrine without an account, your cards, settings, Brand Kit and chat history stay in your browser's storage and are not sent to our database. Product details are still sent to our API and AI providers when you press Generate.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data. Please do not enter personal, financial, health or other sensitive information into product fields, the Brand Kit, support chats or tickets.

## 3. Why we use data and our legal bases
- **Provide the Service** (generate copy, save and sync cards, show your profile): performance of our contract with you; under the DPDP Act, your consent and the legitimate use of providing a service you requested.
- **Accounts and security** (authentication, email verification, password reset, abuse prevention, rate limiting): contract and legitimate interests in keeping the Service secure.
- **Support** (answer questions, resolve tickets): contract and legitimate interests.
- **Legal compliance** (responding to lawful requests, keeping consent records): legal obligation.
- **Improving the Service** (aggregate counts such as number of generations, thumbs-up or thumbs-down feedback stored in your browser): legitimate interests. We do not build advertising profiles.

You give consent by ticking the acceptance box at sign-up and may withdraw it at any time by deleting your account; withdrawal does not affect processing that happened before.

## 4. AI processing
When you generate copy or chat with Ask Kabir, the relevant text is sent from our server to our AI providers: **Groq, Inc.** (primary) and **Google LLC, Gemini API** (fallback). We send only the product details or the question needed for the task, together with instructions; we do not send your email address, account ID or images. **Free tiers of AI services may retain and use inputs and outputs to operate and improve their services, which can include human review.** For this reason we ask you not to submit personal or confidential information. Please review [Groq's privacy policy](https://groq.com/privacy-policy/) and [Google's Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) for their practices.

## 6. International transfers
Our providers may process data in India, the United States, the European Union and other countries. Where the GDPR applies, transfers rely on adequacy decisions or the providers' standard contractual clauses. Under the DPDP Act, transfers are made to countries not restricted by the Government of India.

## 7. Retention
- Account data, cards, settings and Brand Kit: kept while your account is active; deleted when you delete your account, and removed from provider backups within their standard cycles (typically up to 30 days).
- Consent records: kept for the life of the account and up to 12 months after deletion to demonstrate compliance, then deleted.
- Support tickets: kept for up to 24 months after resolution, then deleted.
- Server logs: kept by hosting providers for their standard retention (typically 1 to 30 days).
- Browser storage: stays on your device until you clear it (Settings, History & Privacy, Clear local data).

## 8. Your rights
Depending on where you live, you have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; correct or update it; erase it; port it in a machine-readable format; withdraw consent; object to or restrict certain processing; nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity (DPDP Act); and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (for example the Data Protection Board of India, or your local EU/UK authority).

You can exercise most rights yourself: **Settings, History & Privacy, Export my data** downloads your data as JSON, and **Settings, Account, Delete account** permanently deletes your account and content. For other requests, contact us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. We respond within 30 days and may need to verify your identity.

## 9. Security
We protect data with: HTTPS everywhere; strict security headers and a Content Security Policy; API keys kept only on the server; Firestore security rules that restrict every record to its owner; server-side verification of sign-in tokens; input validation and size limits; rate limiting; output filtering for the support assistant; secret scanning and dependency audits in our build pipeline. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a personal data breach that affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

## 10. Cookies and similar technologies
We use only essential storage by default and never use advertising or analytics cookies. See the [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies).

## 11. Children
Vitrine is not intended for children under 13, and users under 18 need the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly process children's data for tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising. If you believe a child has given us personal data without consent, contact us and we will delete it.
